How it works: feature extraction vs fine-tuning

All three approaches classify the same small images (flowers or satellite tiles) and share the same input scaling, augmentation, classification head, optimiser family and batch size. They differ only in which weights start pretrained and which weights are updated.

Time-to-target is the cumulative wall-clock training time (including the per-epoch validation pass) at the end of the first epoch whose validation accuracy is at least the target. A run that never reaches the target is charged its full budget, so its time is a lower bound. The saving is one minus the mean time of an approach divided by the mean time of training from scratch.
